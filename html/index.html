The archive

From a park visit to a stamp design

Every design traces back to a visit. A park can have more than one visit, and a design can have more than one version. The archive keeps six kinds of record.

1
Park
The starting point. Each park has its own record and can hold several visits.
2
Field observation
What I see and photograph on site.
3
Sketchbook
Sketches and notes made in the field.
4
Visual synthesis
Where observations are pulled together into a visual direction for the park.
5
Cancellation stamp
The design itself, with its versions and any fabrication or prototype work.
6
Field information
Practical details about the visit, kept with the park record.
Project stages

The archive grows in three stages

Stage 1 · Now
Current state
62 parks visited. Designs are in development. Photographs, sketches and files are added as they are organized, so some records are still thin.
Stage 2 · Sabbatical: January–December 2027
Research and development
Field research, design development, refinement, fabrication and documentation continue. Two visits are still to come: American Samoa (Summer 2027) and Chiricahua (2027).
Stage 3 · Plan: Fall 2027
64-park archive
The plan is to draft a first set of all 64 designs. Refinement may follow, so this is a first set, not a final one.
